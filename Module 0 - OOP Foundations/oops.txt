Inheretance
============
why:Imagine you're modeling shapes in a game: Circle, Square, Triangle. They're 
	all different, but they share things in common — every shape has an area, a color, a position. Without inheritance, you'd duplicate that shared stuff in every single class. Inheritance lets you write the shared parts once, in a base class, and have other classes build on top of it.

what:Inheritance lets one class (the derived class) reuse and extend the data/
	 behavior of another class (the base class). The relationship is called "is-a" — a Circle is a Shape.
